    How to pour over

    1. Put a paper filter in the Chemex, with the thick side toward the spout. Pour hot water through it to rinse, then tip that water out. This rinses away any papery taste and warms the glass.
    2. Set the Chemex on a kitchen scale in grams. Add the ground coffee (the setup card shows how much), give it a gentle shake so it sits flat, then zero the scale.
    3. Boil water and let it rest about 30 seconds. Tap Begin brewing.
    4. The bloom: slowly pour just enough water to wet all the coffee. It will puff up and bubble, and that’s a good sign. Let it rest.
    5. The pours: at each chime, pour slowly in gentle circles, starting in the middle and spiraling outward, until the scale reads the number shown.
    6. Drawdown: stop pouring and let it all drain through. When the dripping stops, lift out the filter, swirl, and pour two cups.
    7. Go slow and steady. The gentle, unhurried pour is the whole secret, and it doesn’t need to be perfect to taste wonderful.
