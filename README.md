# chemex2

Chemex brew assistant and timer, paper diorama edition.

A cut-paper Blue Ridge diorama plays out a sunrise over the five-minute brew. Stars and the moon on their threads get pulled away, the fog in the hollows turns pink and lifts, the sawtooth paper sun is hoisted up, and the Chemex on the stump fills. When it's ready, two mugs appear beside it.

- Same brew math as the original: 1:16 ratio, bloom at 2x the coffee weight, then three even pours at 0:45, 1:30 and 2:15, and drawdown from 3:00.
- Soft chime and vibration at each pour (toggle with the bell).
- Keeps the screen awake while the timer runs.
- Remembers your grinder and water amount.
- Installable to the home screen and works offline.

Add `?speed=20` to the URL to preview the whole sunrise quickly.
